This standalone file creates a proforma quotation, commercial invoice and packing list for the same shipment. Data is stored in this file's own local workspace. Export a JSON backup to retain entered shipment records.

  • This copy starts with a blank shipment. Use Sample to explore fictional data, or import your own backup.
  • To register a saved PDF, open Upload saved PDF and use the VPIN printed on that PDF.
  • Enter shared exporter, buyer, product and order details in Proforma Invoice. Commercial Invoice and Packing List link to that shipment automatically.
  • Proforma has its own number, date, validity and choice of tax-exclusive or tax-inclusive quotation.
  • Commercial invoice has final tax mode, payment balance, reverse-charge status and official e-invoice references when applicable.
  • Packing list records each outer package group once. Mixed contents and split kit components are matched against sale quantities.
  • Kg, sheets and sets are totalled by unit. Dimensions use centimetres, weights use kilograms, and volume uses cubic metres.
  • Cerana starts with 8 brood frames and 8 super frames per set. Mellifera starts with 10 and 10. All components, materials and dimensions are editable.
  • The equipment configuration includes floor board, brood chamber, frames, honey super, inner cover, outer roof, queen excluder, entrance reducer, feeder and stand. Optional foundation sheets are recorded separately in the kit contents.
  • Hive sets are empty wooden equipment, without live bees. The set schedule is linked to the sales item and does not add shipping packages.
  • Sample prices, quantities, weights, dimensions and grades are planning examples. Confirm the actual shipment and destination requirements before issue.
  • Honey and raw beeswax reference HSNs are prefilled. Foundation sheets and complete wooden sets need confirmed classification. Every HSN and tax rate is editable and requires confirmation for the commercial Ready workflow.
  • The Ready marker checks entered data and allocation consistency. The official IRN and signed QR image come from an authorised IRP when e-invoicing applies.
  • Download HTML saves the app. Export backup saves the current entered shipment.
