New invoice starts with prefilled company, buyer, bank, terms and signature details. You can edit every field before saving or printing.

Print / Save PDF gives the sharpest result: choose A4, Margins None, Scale 100%, and switch Background graphics on.

Export settings is your backup. Draft changes are saved in this browser. Use Save Invoice to save the invoice data and signature securely to the cloud. Later choose Open Saved Invoice, search by number or buyer, and open it to edit or download its PDF. PDFs are generated from the saved data when you download them; a separate PDF file is not stored in the cloud.
