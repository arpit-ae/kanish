Frequently asked questions

Before you get in touch

What does USOA GROUP deal in?

USOA GROUP deals in honey, bee keeping, bee colonies buy and sell, bee equipment and bee products, along with wholesale and export-import enquiries.

Can I make a wholesale enquiry?

Yes. Send the product, approximate quantity and delivery location through WhatsApp, phone or email.

Can I enquire about bee colonies?

Yes. USOA GROUP's stated business scope includes bee colony buy and sell enquiries. Availability and applicable terms should be confirmed directly.

Can I enquire about bee equipment?

Yes. Enquiries can be made for bee boxes, honey extractors, gloves and other bee equipment or bee products.

Does USOA GROUP handle export and import?

Export-import is included in the stated business scope. For any transaction, product eligibility, documentation, destination requirements and applicable laws should be confirmed before proceeding.
