# USOA Billing v4.0-alpha.1 — Foundation
Development branch only. Not an approved production release.

Implemented in source: encrypted archive payloads in Android SQLite, AES-256-GCM with Android Keystore, keyed HMAC indexes, transactional archive import, v3 archive migration, local duplicate-number guard, local device-series numbering allocator, domestic calculation bounds, account binding and serialized sync within one process. Original five billing formats retained. Fiscal numbering and calculation regression tests in CI.

Limitations: original v3 plaintext preferences remain for recovery and WebView drafts still use localStorage; encrypted draft table is present but not connected. Cloud/manual JSON backups still use v3 record format without app-level encryption; recovery-key encryption, PIN/biometric and SQLCipher full-database encryption are pending. No guarantee of globally unique offline numbers until device series are centrally coordinated; manual series assignment is a development feature. Existing/imported duplicate numbers are retained and counted; same-ID conflicts across devices are not reconciled. Issuance/correction workflow and device series recovery are pending. The two legacy editors still use one current draft per module. Customer/product masters, payment/stock ledgers, reports, conflict UI and real Android testing remain. Do not use alpha for live GST issuance. Source does not change main website or existing database. Debug/unsigned CI output must not replace signed v3.0 on a live phone. No signing material is committed.
